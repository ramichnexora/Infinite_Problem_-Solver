# Product Proposal: The Toddler Sleep Regression Fix

## Why this, and why it's distinct from the Postpartum Sleep Survival Handbook

Reviewed the existing handbook directly (28 pages, `pdf_guide/postpartum-sleep-survival-handbook-v3.pdf`):
it is a strong, real product — hormone/physiology explanation, the Sleep Banking
Method, a 14-day recovery plan — but its entire scope is the **newborn stage**
(0–3 months, "fourth trimester").

Every parent who buys that handbook hits a second, equally brutal sleep crisis
6–24 months later: the 4-month/8-month/18-month regressions, the 2-nap-to-1-nap
transition, and the toddler bedtime standoff. Almost no guide treats this stage
with the same seriousness — most "toddler sleep" content is vague listicles.
That gap is the product, not a rehash of the newborn playbook.

This does NOT compete with or duplicate the existing product — it is the
natural next purchase for the same customer 6+ months later, and can be
cross-sold on the Postpartum Handbook's own product page later.

## Spec

**Title:** The Toddler Sleep Regression Fix

**Angle:** A parent survived the newborn stage, sleep finally stabilized — then
at 8, 12, or 18 months it fell apart again, and nobody warned them why or what
to do about it.

**Target audience:** Parents of children 6 months–3 years going through a
sleep regression, nap transition, or bedtime resistance phase. Likely repeat
buyers from the Postpartum Handbook's audience, 6–18 months later.

**Suggested price:** $69 (slightly below the $79 Postpartum Handbook — same
value tier, distinct product, avoids implying it's "more advanced/expensive")

**Price justification:** Cheaper than one hour of a sleep consultant's time
(commonly $150–300/session), reusable across every regression and every future
child, no recurring cost.

**What's inside (concrete, not vague):**
- Why regressions happen: object permanence, separation anxiety, teething,
  and developmental leaps mapped to age (4mo, 8-10mo, 12mo, 18mo, 2yr)
- The 2-to-1 nap transition: a step-by-step schedule shift over 1-2 weeks,
  not a cold switch
- Bedtime resistance / stalling: scripts for the "one more" negotiations
  without giving in or escalating a power struggle
- A "Regression vs. Something Else" checklist — teething, illness, growth
  spurt, or an actual habit that needs resetting
- 14-day reset plan (same proven structure as the Postpartum Handbook, applied
  to this stage)
- Blank tracking worksheet (nap times, wake windows, bedtime resistance log)

**Tags:** toddler sleep, sleep regression, nap transition, toddler bedtime,
parenting, sleep training, 18 month sleep regression, 2 to 1 nap transition

existing 14-day satisfaction guarantee (per the refund policy update made
2026-09-08), not left as a separate/different policy.
