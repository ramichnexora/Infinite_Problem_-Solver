# Decision: no money spent on Shopify billing until real sales arrive

**Date:** 2026-09-16
**Decided by:** founder (direct instruction in the MILI session)

## What happened

Shopify's Advanced-plan bill payment of $399.00 USD failed on 2026-09-16
(08:49 UTC, `billing@shopify.com`). Flagged on the MILI Command Deck
dashboard as a "Needs founder action" item. Founder was asked whether to
retry/fix the payment method.

## Decision

Founder will not spend any money to keep the Shopify subscription current
("যতক্ষণ না তার সম্পূর্ণ সেল আসবে ততক্ষণ পর্যন্ত সেখানে এর একটি পয়সাও ব্যয়
করব না" — not a single taka spent there until real sales actually come
in). Store gross sales are still $0 over the last 30 days; no real paying
customer exists yet.

## What this means for MILI / any agent or automation

- Do **not** propose paying, retrying, or upgrading Shopify billing as a
  next step. That decision is explicitly deferred until real revenue
  exists.
- The failed-payment item stays flagged on the dashboard as informational
  (Shopify will keep retrying on its own schedule and may eventually
  restrict/suspend the store — report that if/when it happens), but no
  agent should nudge the founder to act on it before real sales land.
- This does not change or reverse the plan-downgrade / pause-and-build
  options discussed earlier — those remain available if the founder wants
  to act, but nothing here is authorization to spend on the founder's
  behalf, ever.
- If the store does get restricted/suspended as a result, that is an
  accepted consequence of this decision, not a bug to silently "fix" by
  spending money.

## Follow-up

None required right now. Revisit only once real sales exist, or if the
founder raises it again.
