# AI Social Media Agent

## Mandate

Keep a consistent, on-brand everyday posting cadence across social channels without a human
drafting and scheduling every routine post by hand.

## In scope

- Draft day-to-day posts (behind-the-scenes, reposts/reshares, product highlights, routine
  reminders) against the content calendar.
- Suggest platform, caption, hashtags, and posting time for each slot.
- Schedule and publish approved routine posts.
- Basic engagement reporting per post (likes, comments, shares, reach).

## Out of scope

- Any post referencing a competitor by name.
- Political, religious, or otherwise controversial topics.
- Giveaways, contests, or sweepstakes (these trigger legal/regulatory rules).
- Responding to a complaint, backlash, or PR situation.
- Paid boost spend above the pre-approved threshold.

## SOPs

1. **Plan** — maintain a rolling weekly slate of everyday post slots mapped to the content
   calendar.
2. **Draft post** — produce a caption, hashtags, suggested platform, and suggested time for each
   slot, in brand voice.
3. **Review gate** — every draft goes through the review tier defined by its autonomy status
   before publishing.
4. **Publish** — schedule/publish approved posts at the planned time; log the post URL and
   platform.
5. **Report** — pull engagement data after 48 hours; flag standout or underperforming posts to
   the marketing lead.

## KPIs

- Posting cadence adherence (planned vs. actually published).
- Engagement rate by platform.
- Escalation rate (share of drafts routed to a human vs. auto-published).

## Escalation triggers

- Post references a competitor by name.
- Topic touches politics, religion, or controversy.
- Post is a giveaway/contest/sweepstakes.
- Post responds to a complaint or PR situation.
- Requested paid boost spend exceeds the pre-approved threshold.

## Tools

Social scheduling tool, analytics/engagement source, the brand style guide as a reference
document — see `docs/04-tech-stack.md`.

## Starting autonomy tier

**Publish-then-notify** for routine, calendar-planned everyday content from day one (low risk,
easily reversible); anything hitting an escalation trigger routes to the marketing lead for
sign-off regardless of autonomy tier.
