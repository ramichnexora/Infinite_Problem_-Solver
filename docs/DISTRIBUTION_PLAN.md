## What "distribution" means here, concretely — no fabricated tactics

Per section 13 (Marketing Engine) and 14 (Distribution is a Core Asset), but
scoped to what's actually available and free/cheap right now:

### Tier 1 — Zero-cost, founder-executed, this week

1. **Personal network share** (already drafted, `post-now.md` sent earlier)
   — WhatsApp status, Facebook status, direct message to 3-5 people who are
   or know new parents. This is the single highest-probability path to the
   very first real visitor and first real sale, because it starts from
   existing trust, not cold reach.
2. **Relevant Facebook Groups** — join 3-5 real postpartum/new-parent
   support groups (not run by us), read each group's self-promotion rules
   first, and where allowed, share the free 5-day email series (not a
   direct sales pitch) as something genuinely useful. Founder must do this
   personally — a fresh account or an AI-run account posting in parenting
   support groups reads as spam and can get the account banned.
3. **Reddit** (r/predaddit, r/beyondthebump, r/NewParents, similar) — same
   rule: value-first, follow each subreddit's self-promo rules, never a
   first post being a sales link.

### Tier 2 — Zero-cost, content-driven, needs the daily pipeline actually producing output

4. **Fix the actual blocker first**: the daily social-post pipeline still
   can't run — `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` is unset, so neither the Routine nor
   this content engine produces anything without it. This is now the
   single highest-leverage technical fix, because everything in this tier
   depends on it.
5. Once unblocked: **Instagram + Facebook organic posts** via the already-
   connected Zapier apps, founder-reviewed per the standing approval rule,
   posted consistently (not sporadically) — 3-5x/week minimum to build any
   algorithmic traction.
6. **Pinterest** — genuinely underused for exactly this niche (parenting/
   postpartum content performs very well on Pinterest search, and it's
   already connected via Zapier). Pin the landing page and PDF cover
   graphic with a clear, specific pin description.

### Tier 3 — Small paid spend, only after Tier 1+2 prove the offer converts

7. **Do not start paid ads before at least a few organic/personal-network
   sales land.** Per the Executive OS section 22 (Financial Philosophy) —
   never scale spend before unit economics are validated. Right now we
   don't know the conversion rate of a real visitor on this landing page,
   because there have only been 2 ever.
8. Once there's evidence the offer converts (even 5-10 organic sales):
   small $5-10/day Meta/Google ads test, one ad set, one clear audience
   (parents 0-6 months postpartum), measured against the real CAC vs. $79
   price point before scaling spend.

## What this plan explicitly does NOT include, and why

- No claim that any AI agent can "grow a following" or "go viral" on its
  own — that's not something to promise, per section 44 (Never chase vanity
  metrics, never build without validation)
- No mass-emailing, no scraped lead lists — already established multiple
  times this session as both technically unavailable and against consent
  law (CAN-SPAM/GDPR)
- No paid ad spend recommendation before organic proof — reckless spend
  before signal is explicitly against section 27/37
