# Support Knowledge Base (sample)

## Resetting your password
Go to Settings > Security > Reset Password. A reset link is emailed within 5 minutes.
If it doesn't arrive, check spam, then confirm the account email is correct.

## Understanding your invoice
Invoices are generated on the 1st of each month and reflect usage from the prior
calendar month. Line items are broken out by product tier and any usage overages.

## Changing your plan
Plan changes take effect immediately for upgrades and at the next billing cycle for
downgrades. Downgrades cannot be reversed mid-cycle.

## Exporting your data
Go to Settings > Data > Export. Exports are generated asynchronously and emailed as a
download link within 30 minutes, valid for 7 days.

## Standard refund policy
Refunds under $100 for billing errors (double charge, incorrect proration) can be
issued directly. Anything above that, or refunds for reasons other than a billing
error, requires escalation to the finance/support lead.
