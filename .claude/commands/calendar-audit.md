name: calendar-audit
description: Sunday Calendar & Energy Audit — DRIP matrix, 2x2 truth grid, next week's Perfect Week.
---

Run the `coo` lead agent, delegating to `time-energy-auditor`.

1. **DRIP matrix** — sort last week's real time-spend (from the founder's
   actual calendar/notes, not an assumption) into Delegate / Replace /
   Improve / Preserve.
2. **2x2 truth grid** — plot last week's tasks on Energy (gives/drains) x
   Skill (genius/competent), honestly, even where it's uncomfortable.
3. **Next week's Perfect Week** — propose a week structure that shifts time
   toward the Preserve/genius quadrant and delegates or automates everything
   in Delegate/Replace, citing the specific sub-agent or automation that
   would take it over.
4. Write the result to `knowledge/calendar-audits/<date>.md`.
5. Flag anything that needs a real decision from the founder (a hire, a
   subscription, a new automation) rather than silently deciding it yourself.
